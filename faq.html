Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated 25 October 2026

Answers about ordering, delivery, sizing and caring for your Azuri experience.

How do I place an order?

Choose a colour and an available size on the product page, add it to your bag, then complete checkout. Review your contact details, address and total before selecting “Place order”. You do not need a customer account.

Does checkout take payment?

Not currently. Checkout sends an order request and does not charge a card. Azuri will contact you to confirm availability, payment arrangements and delivery. The automatic email acknowledges your request; it is not a payment receipt or final acceptance of the sale.

Will I receive an order confirmation?

After a successful request, the website shows an order reference and sends an acknowledgement to the email address you entered. Keep that reference. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and contact azuriclothingtt@gmail.com before submitting the same order again.

Where do you deliver, and what does it cost?

We currently deliver within Trinidad and Tobago only. Trinidad delivery fees range from TT$40 to TT$80 according to the area, and Tobago is TT$80 islandwide. Your checkout total includes the delivery fee once your town or city is recognised.

Do I have to choose a delivery zone?

No. Enter your complete street address and town or city; checkout calculates the rate automatically. Town suggestions help with spelling. If your actual delivery location is not recognised, contact us for help rather than selecting an unrelated town.

How long will delivery take?

We will contact you to arrange delivery and confirm an estimate for your order. The website does not currently promise a fixed delivery time. See Shipping & Returns for the current delivery information.

What sizes are available, and how do I choose?

Élan is a slim fit and is offered in S, M, L and XL, subject to stock. Open the Size & Fit Guide on the product page. Its measurements are taken from the garment in centimetres; ½ chest is the width across a garment laid flat, not your body’s chest circumference.

What is Élan made from?

Élan uses 250 GSM fine 1×1 rib fabric made from 97% combed long-staple cotton and 3% elastane. The product page includes the full details and care instructions.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. We will confirm what can be changed based on the order’s status. If it has already been dispatched, the return process may apply.

Can I return an item or exchange a size?

Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of delivery under our Shipping & Returns policy. For a voluntary return, keep the item unworn and unused, with its tags and original packaging. Contact us before sending it back. Size exchanges depend on availability.

What if my item is faulty, incorrect or not as described?

Contact azuriclothingtt@gmail.com with your order reference and a description of the issue. Relevant photographs can help us assess it. The conditions for voluntary returns do not remove your consumer rights for faulty, incorrect or misdescribed goods.

How are refunds handled?

If a refund is due, we will confirm the amount, the appropriate payment method and the expected processing time with you. Timing can depend on the payment method and financial institution. Your applicable consumer rights are unaffected.

Why has my review not appeared yet?

Reviews are checked before publication. Honest positive and negative feedback is welcome. We may withhold spam, unlawful content, private information or misleading material. A published review is not automatically a verified-purchase review.

Will ordering sign me up for marketing emails?

No. An order request is used for order-related communication, not a promotional subscription. Read our Privacy Policy for how customer information is handled.

Still need a hand?

Email azuriclothingtt@gmail.com or call (868) 434-8695. Please include your order reference if your question concerns an order.
