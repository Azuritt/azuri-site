Shipping & Returns

Everything you need to know about receiving and returning your Azuri order.

01 — SHIPPING

Shipping

Where we ship

Azuri currently ships within Trinidad & Tobago.

Processing

Orders are carefully prepared after purchase. You will receive confirmation once your order has been processed.

Delivery

Delivery times and shipping fees are confirmed during checkout.

02 — RETURNS

Returns & Exchanges

Returns

Eligible items may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Items must be:

  • Unworn and unused
  • In original condition
  • With all original tags attached
  • In the original packaging

Exchanges

If you need a different size, contact Azuri with your order number and we will assist you with the exchange process.

Non-returnable items

Items that have been worn, washed, damaged, or altered may not be eligible for return.

03 — QUESTIONS?

Need help?

For shipping or return questions, contact Azuri at azuriclothingtt@gmail.com or (868) 434-8695.
