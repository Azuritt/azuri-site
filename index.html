Born in Trinidad & Tobago, Azuri starts with a simple idea: clothes that speak for themselves.

Clean lines, considered details and a slim silhouette. Élan brings a refined ease to your everyday wardrobe.

The details of Élan

The fabric.

250 GSM long-staple cotton with a touch of stretch. A fine 1×1 rib with a smooth, matte finish.

The silhouette.

A slim fit, an open neckline and ribbed cuffs. Clean proportions, made for everyday wear.

The signature.

Subtle Azuri embroidery. A considered finishing touch that lets the piece speak for itself.
